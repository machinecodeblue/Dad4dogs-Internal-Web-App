# Decision

**Status:** accepted (2026-08-24) — partial
**Live spec:** `platform.md` **Detail screens (labeled cards)** — standing policy for customer, dog, and any future detail screen. Also `customers.md` screen table.
**What we took:** Every customer-summary card is labeled by **job**: **Primary owner contact**, **Emergency & Pickups**, **Dogs**. Owner Call sits beside the phone number, not a full-width block under the name. Emergency person is one unit: name + “Emergency contact” + yellow Call. Authorized pickup is a labeled list on that same card. COI stays on the primary card (✓ or missing warning).
**What we left:** SMS/Text next to Call. A second Edit on the emergency card (one Edit still opens the existing form). Icon-only 📞 / 📍 buttons — Call/Map stay as labeled controls.
**Why:** Untitled cards and giant stacked buttons made it unclear what each panel was for, and disconnected the emergency person’s name from their number.
**Do not edit this file to change product rules.** Change `customers.md` / `platform.md` instead.

---

This is a classic case of UI elements fighting for attention. When everything is huge and visually dominant (like those massive Call buttons), nothing stands out, and the relationship between the data gets lost.

Here is a breakdown of how to restructure this interface to make it highly scannable, mobile-friendly (without being comically oversized), and logically grouped.

### Key Flow Improvements

* **Standardized Touch Targets:** Instead of massive, full-width blocks of color, use standardized icon buttons (like a phone icon `[ 📞 ]` or a map pin `[ 📍 ]`) placed directly next to the relevant data. This is still perfectly tappable on a screen but saves massive amounts of vertical space.
* **The Principle of Proximity:** In your current setup, David Lundquist's name and phone number feel disconnected because of the awkward spacing and button size. By grouping the name, their specific role ("Emergency Contact" or "Authorized Pickup"), and their phone number tightly together, the brain instantly processes them as one unit.
* **Surfacing Authorized Pickups:** By renaming the second card to "Alternate Contacts" (or similar), you create a natural home for both Emergency Contacts and Authorized Pickups. They should be listed side-by-side or stacked with clear labels so they are never treated as an afterthought.
* **Consolidated Admin:** Moving the COI status directly into the bottom of Kathleen's primary profile card removes the need for a standalone, floating UI element for a single administrative toggle.
